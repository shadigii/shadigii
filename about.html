I'm Shafran, and together with my friend, I built Sha Digii after spending years working in an international digital marketing company. During that experience, we saw how difficult it could be for local businesses to access professional digital marketing services within a realistic budget.

That led us to create Sha Digii — with packages designed to make professional digital support more accessible, whether you're running a home-based business or building a growing brand.

For the past 2 years, our team has been helping businesses build and manage their online presence while focusing on what matters beyond likes and views: revenue, performance, and real business impact.

From content and social media to advertising, websites, customer inquiries, and online sales support, we work alongside businesses to take care of the digital side while they focus on growing their business.
